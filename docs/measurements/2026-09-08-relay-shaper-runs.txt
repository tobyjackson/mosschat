WO-1.3c relay shaper: 20 runs of relay_path_carries_10_mib_unchanged
====================================================================

What this is. The mechanism proof section 8's WO-1.3c asks for: 20 runs of
`gate::relay_path_carries_10_mib_unchanged`, one at a time, each under 15 s
of wall clock, and at the end of every run `relay_rate_limited`,
`relay_dropped_at_full` and `relay_socket_backpressure` all 0. The counter
assertions are in the test itself, so CI enforces them; this file is the
wall clock record, which CI does not keep.

When and where. 2026-09-08, finishing at 06:32 local time, timezone
MDT (UTC-6), on a MacBook (Darwin 25.6.0 arm64), branch
phase-1/wo-1.3c-shaper at the commit this file lands in. Nothing else
running: no other build, no other test, no synthetic load.

How. `cargo test -p mosschat-net --test gate --no-run` first, then the
already-built harness invoked directly 20 times in sequence, each as
`nice -n 10 target/debug/deps/gate-<hash> --exact
gate::relay_path_carries_10_mib_unchanged --nocapture --test-threads 1`.
The time is wall clock around the whole process, measured with a monotonic
clock in the driver, so it includes process start and teardown as well as
the test body. Compilation is not in it, and neither is any other test in
the file.

Result. 20 of 20 passed. Min 5.00 s, median 5.00 s, max 5.12 s, against
the 15 s ceiling and against the 120.96 s, 124.02 s and 126.31 s wedges
this test produced on `main` and on macOS CI before the shaper. Every run
ended with the gate's `relay_rate_limited` and `relay_dropped_at_full` at
0 and both houses' `relay_socket_backpressure` at 0.

Reading the counter line. `gate queued` is both directions of the session
as the gate saw them; `alice` is the 10 MiB sender and `bob` the receiver,
each reporting its own house side egress queue. Alice's p50 of about
49.9 ms is the shaper working as section 1 describes rather than a fault:
a bulk sender fills its 100 deep queue, 100 datagrams at 2000 a second is
50 ms, and every datagram then waits the depth of the queue. The gate's
p50 of 4 to 8 microseconds is the other half of the same picture: a house
that paces at 2000 a second never builds a queue at a gate draining at
2200.
